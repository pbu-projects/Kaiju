*Bio & Context:*
Dr. Sarah oversees court-mandated youth mentoring, transitional foster youth programs, and a 24/7 crisis support line. Her programs involve direct contact with vulnerable minors and individuals in trauma. She faces strict legal mandates (state child protection laws, HIPAA/privacy rules, liability insurance) and cannot permit unvetted public sign-ups. She requires rigorous screening, background check tracking, and certified training verifications before shift booking is unlocked.

*Needs & Core Goals:*
* Enforce mandatory qualification gates (FBI/DOJ background check, 40h crisis training) before any volunteer accesses shifts.
* Track expiration dates of background clearances and professional licenses automatically with zero compliance lapses.
* Manage a structured onboarding pipeline: Application -> Background Check -> Training -> Supervised Shift -> Qualified Roster.
* Maintain an immutable, audit-ready compliance ledger of every volunteer's credentials, supervisor approvals, and client contact hours.

*Frustrations & Obstacles:*
* Public platforms allowing unvetted walk-ins to self-register for sensitive youth or shelter shifts without screening.
* Tracking background check renewal dates across 150 volunteers in messy spreadsheets, creating extreme liability risk.
* High drop-out rates midway through the 40-hour training curriculum, wasting hundreds of dollars in non-profit vetting fees.
* Lack of granular permissions to restrict sensitive case notes and client addresses from general volunteers.
