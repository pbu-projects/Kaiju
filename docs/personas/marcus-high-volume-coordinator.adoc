*Bio & Context:*

Marcus manages volunteer logistics for a metropolitan food rescue and urban agriculture non-profit. He coordinates over 600 monthly volunteer slots across 5 physical distribution warehouses and seasonal farm gleaning sites. Operating with a minimal staff, he spends excessive hours re-entering data between paper clipboards, Excel sheets, and municipal permits while battling a frustrating 30–50% volunteer no-show rate.

*Needs & Core Goals:*

* Fill 100% of shift capacity for critical assembly lines (e.g. Saturday emergency food boxes).
* Eliminate the chronic 35% volunteer no-show rate through automated waitlists and reminders.
* Manage multi-site initiatives under a single umbrella project without cloning identical records.
* Maintain digital records of signed liability waivers and emergency contacts to shield the non-profit.

*Frustrations & Obstacles:*

* Volunteers RSVPing weeks in advance and ghosting on Saturday morning, starving packaging lines.
* Spreadsheet desynchronization between office coordinators and on-site warehouse managers.
* Re-typing the same project description, safety rules, and requirements 10 times for 10 distribution hubs.
* Spending hours manually generating PDF service verification letters for high school students.
