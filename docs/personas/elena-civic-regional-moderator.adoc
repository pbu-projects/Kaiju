*Bio & Context:*
Elena oversees municipal volunteer services, community engagement initiatives, and public safety vetting across a regional jurisdiction covering four municipalities. She is legally responsible for ensuring that all published volunteer opportunities adhere to municipal safety codes, insurance requirements, and non-profit validity. She must balance thorough civic oversight with strict 48-hour SLA turnaround times to avoid stalling grassroots initiatives.

*Needs & Core Goals:*
* Safeguard public trust by intercepting fraudulent projects, commercial marketing scams, or hazardous tasks.
* Clear all pending regional project reviews within the mandatory 48-hour SLA window.
* Ensure regional boundaries are strictly respected so emergency services and municipal liability are clear.
* Maintain an immutable, court-admissible audit trail of all project approvals, rejections, and revisions.

*Frustrations & Obstacles:*
* Vague project submissions missing basic safety protocols, insurance proof, or accurate physical coordinates.
* Grassroots organizers making unauthorized edits to project descriptions after civic approval has been granted.
* Submissions straddling municipal boundaries creating confusion over emergency service jurisdictions.
* Review queue bottlenecks during peak disaster relief or holiday seasons threatening 48-hour SLA compliance.
