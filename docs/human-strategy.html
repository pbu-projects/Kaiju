This specification establishes the comprehensive Human Strategy for Kaiju (Volunteer Monster), grounding its Phase 1 PostGIS spatial foundation in real human behaviors, motivations, and operational constraints. Adhering to Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) best practices, this document models 7 Proto-Personas and their 4-Quadrant Empathy Maps (Says, Thinks, Does, Feels), addressing the dual spectrums of volunteerism: Spontaneous vs. Skilled & Academic/Mandated Volunteers, alongside High-Volume vs. High-Liability Non-Profit Programs.

Executive Summary: The Dual Human Spectrums & The Domain Chasm

As synthesized in the architectural evaluation, the Kaiju platform has achieved an elite foundation in Phase 1: Spatial Discovery & Core Data. Powered by PostgreSQL 17, PostGIS GEOGRAPHY(Point, 4326) and Polygon indexing, Fowler’s Single Table Inheritance (STI), and Micronaut 5 compile-time dependency injection, the system reliably answers:

"Where is an initiative located, what type of opportunity is it, and within which civic boundary does it reside?"

However, an enterprise volunteer ecosystem is governed by two distinct human spectrums:

1. The Volunteer Spectrum (Motivations & Commitments)

  1. Spontaneous / Proximity-First Volunteers (Maya Lin): Seeking low-friction, 2-to-3-hour opportunities near home. High abandonment risk if faced with login walls, paperwork, or mobile lag.

  2. Purpose-Driven & Skill-Building Specialists (David Chen): Seeking cause alignment (#wildlife, #climate) and professional skill utilization (#gis, #data-analysis). Willing to complete background checks, attend orientations, and commit to multi-month recurring schedules.

  3. Academic & Portfolio-Building Students (Chloe Torres): Seeking 100+ verified service hours for college admissions, National Honor Society, or club philanthropy. Requires group slot reservations and official, accredited PDF transcripts.

  4. Compliance & Mandated Volunteers (Raymond Miller): Fulfilling court-ordered community restitution or SNAP ABAWD monthly work requirements. Requires explicit court/SNAP eligibility filters, GPS-verified check-in/out timestamps, and tamper-proof legal reporting to avoid probation violations or benefit loss.

2. The Non-Profit Coordinator Spectrum (Volume vs. Liability)

  1. High-Volume Operations Coordinators (Marcus Vance): Managing food banks, community cleanups, and warehouse packaging lines. Battles a chronic 30–50% volunteer no-show rate and needs fast multi-site shift coordination.

  2. Specialized & High-Liability Program Directors (Dr. Sarah Al-Mansoor): Managing youth mentorship, foster care, and 24/7 crisis support lines. Legally cannot allow "just anyone off the street" near vulnerable populations. Requires mandatory background check vetting, certified training gates, and credential expiration tracking.

3. The Civic Governance Authority

  • Civic Regional Moderators (Elena Rostova): Municipal leaders responsible for public safety, territorial boundary integrity, and 48-hour SLA turnaround.

Bridging the Domain Chasm

Phase 1 established the Spatial Catalog. Phases 2, 3, and 4 must construct the Human Half: Capacity controls, automated waitlists, digital liability waivers, on-site QR attendance, and verified volunteer hour credentials.

Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) Framework & Methodology

According to Nielsen Norman Group research standards: * Proto-Personas: Assumption-based archetypes synthesizing existing domain knowledge, organizational context, and behavioral observations before formal field studies. * 4-Quadrant Empathy Maps: Capture a single user persona’s current-state experience: SAYS: Direct verbal statements, quotes, and explicit questions vocalized aloud. THINKS: Unspoken anxieties, doubts, preoccupations, and internalized beliefs. DOES: Concrete physical actions, workflows, workarounds, and observable habits. FEELS: Emotional states, affective adjectives, cognitive fatigue, and emotional triumphs. PAINS & GAINS**: Synthesized obstacles (risks, wasted time) vs. desired outcomes (success metrics, relief).

Frustrations & Obstacles: * Clunky non-profit websites taking 8+ seconds to load on mobile cellular browsers. * Being forced to submit a 5-page personal profile just to check donation drop-off hours. * Arriving at a venue only to discover the event was quietly rescheduled, cancelled, or overbooked. * Inaccurate map pins pointing to administrative headquarters rather than the actual work site.

NN/g 4-Quadrant Empathy Map

🗣️ SAYS (Explicit Verbal Expressions)

  • "I have three free hours this Saturday morning—is there anything close to me?"

  • "Can someone officially verify my 20 hours for my university service requirement?"

  • "Do I need to bring work gloves, or does the organization provide all safety gear?"

  • "I don’t want to create an account with a password just to see what time donations are accepted."

💭 THINKS (Unspoken Beliefs & Doubts)

  • I hope this isn’t disorganized like the last cleanup where we stood around for 45 minutes doing nothing.

  • Is this neighborhood safe to walk to early on a Saturday? Where do I safely park?

  • Will anyone actually notice or care if I show up, or am I just an extra body in a corner?

  • I really want to help, but I cannot commit to a recurring 6-month weekly contract.

⚡ DOES (Observable Behaviors)

  • Searches for volunteer opportunities on her smartphone while riding transit.

  • Toggles between Google Maps and transit apps to verify travel time before committing.

  • Picks OPEN_DOOR walk-in drives during exam weeks and scheduled shifts when calendar permits.

  • Abandons registration flows immediately if blocked by an aggressive login wall before seeing details.

❤️ FEELS (Affective Emotional States)

  • Eager: Genuinely motivated to make an authentic community impact and meet neighborhood peers.

  • Anxious: Apprehensive about arrival logistics, unfamiliar venues, and parking confusion.

  • Impatient: Irritated by sluggish web interfaces, infinite spinners, and redundant forms.

  • Proud: Uplifted and empowered when her volunteer contribution produces tangible community results.

🚨 CRITICAL PAINS & RISKS

  • Phantom Shifts: Traveling across town only to find a cancelled event or an overbooked room.

  • Mobile Latency: Multi-megabyte JS/Wasm bundles freezing mobile browsers on transit.

  • Inaccurate Geocoding: Map pins pointing to corporate offices rather than active work sites.

🎯 DESIRED GAINS & SUCCESS METRICS

  • Sub-50ms Discovery: Frictionless public web discovery with zero JavaScript bundle overhead.

  • Truthful Driving Distance: Real PostGIS geodesic distance with clear site entry instructions.

  • Verified Digital Credential: One-click certified service hour records for university portals.

🛠️ Kaiju Tech Stack Alignment for Maya Lin (24)

  • JTE Server-Side Templates: Compiles templates directly to Java bytecode, delivering sub-50ms TTFB and 0MB JavaScript download weight on mobile transit.

  • PostGIS ST_DWithin Radius: Calculates truthful geodesic distance from Maya’s coordinates (GEOGRAPHY Point, 4326); eliminates fake boundary pins.

  • STI Project Discriminator: Automatically renders the correct UX: OPEN_DOOR provides directions/hours; STANDARD delivers a calendar shift picker.

  • ProjectSearchCard DTO: An immutable, compact @Serdeable record transmitting only 5 essential fields over the wire to maximize mobile battery and data efficiency.

Bio & Context: Elena oversees municipal volunteer services, community engagement initiatives, and public safety vetting across a regional jurisdiction covering four municipalities. She is legally responsible for ensuring that all published volunteer opportunities adhere to municipal safety codes, insurance requirements, and non-profit validity. She must balance thorough civic oversight with strict 48-hour SLA turnaround times to avoid stalling grassroots initiatives.

Needs & Core Goals: * Safeguard public trust by intercepting fraudulent projects, commercial marketing scams, or hazardous tasks. * Clear all pending regional project reviews within the mandatory 48-hour SLA window. * Ensure regional boundaries are strictly respected so emergency services and municipal liability are clear. * Maintain an immutable, court-admissible audit trail of all project approvals, rejections, and revisions.

Frustrations & Obstacles: * Vague project submissions missing basic safety protocols, insurance proof, or accurate physical coordinates. * Grassroots organizers making unauthorized edits to project descriptions after civic approval has been granted. * Submissions straddling municipal boundaries creating confusion over emergency service jurisdictions. * Review queue bottlenecks during peak disaster relief or holiday seasons threatening 48-hour SLA compliance.
