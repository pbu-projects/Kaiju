This specification establishes the comprehensive **Human Strategy** for Kaiju (Volunteer Monster), grounding its Phase 1 PostGIS spatial foundation in real human behaviors, motivations, and operational constraints. Adhering to **Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g)** best practices, this document models 7 Proto-Personas and their 4-Quadrant Empathy Maps (Says, Thinks, Does, Feels), addressing the dual spectrums of volunteerism: Spontaneous vs. Skilled & Academic/Mandated Volunteers, alongside High-Volume vs. High-Liability Non-Profit Programs.

== Executive Summary: The Dual Human Spectrums & The Domain Chasm

As synthesized in the architectural evaluation, the Kaiju platform has achieved an elite foundation in **Phase 1: Spatial Discovery & Core Data**. Powered by PostgreSQL 17, PostGIS `GEOGRAPHY(Point, 4326)` and `Polygon` indexing, Fowler's Single Table Inheritance (STI), and Micronaut 5 compile-time dependency injection, the system reliably answers:
[quote]
"Where is an initiative located, what type of opportunity is it, and within which civic boundary does it reside?"

However, an enterprise volunteer ecosystem is governed by **two distinct human spectrums**:

=== 1. The Volunteer Spectrum (Motivations & Commitments)
1. **Spontaneous / Proximity-First Volunteers (Maya Lin)**: Seeking low-friction, 2-to-3-hour opportunities near home. High abandonment risk if faced with login walls, paperwork, or mobile lag.
2. **Purpose-Driven & Skill-Building Specialists (David Chen)**: Seeking cause alignment (`#wildlife`, `#climate`) and professional skill utilization (`#gis`, `#data-analysis`). Willing to complete background checks, attend orientations, and commit to multi-month recurring schedules.
3. **Academic & Portfolio-Building Students (Chloe Torres)**: Seeking 100+ verified service hours for college admissions, National Honor Society, or club philanthropy. Requires group slot reservations and official, accredited PDF transcripts.
4. **Compliance & Mandated Volunteers (Raymond Miller)**: Fulfilling court-ordered community restitution or SNAP ABAWD monthly work requirements. Requires explicit court/SNAP eligibility filters, GPS-verified check-in/out timestamps, and tamper-proof legal reporting to avoid probation violations or benefit loss.

=== 2. The Non-Profit Coordinator Spectrum (Volume vs. Liability)
1. **High-Volume Operations Coordinators (Marcus Vance)**: Managing food banks, community cleanups, and warehouse packaging lines. Battles a chronic 30–50% volunteer no-show rate and needs fast multi-site shift coordination.
2. **Specialized & High-Liability Program Directors (Dr. Sarah Al-Mansoor)**: Managing youth mentorship, foster care, and 24/7 crisis support lines. Legally cannot allow "just anyone off the street" near vulnerable populations. Requires mandatory background check vetting, certified training gates, and credential expiration tracking.

=== 3. The Civic Governance Authority
* **Civic Regional Moderators (Elena Rostova)**: Municipal leaders responsible for public safety, territorial boundary integrity, and 48-hour SLA turnaround.

[IMPORTANT]
.Bridging the Domain Chasm
====
Phase 1 established the *Spatial Catalog*. Phases 2, 3, and 4 must construct the *Human Half*: Capacity controls, automated waitlists, digital liability waivers, on-site QR attendance, and verified volunteer hour credentials.
====

== Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) Framework & Methodology

According to Nielsen Norman Group research standards:
* **Proto-Personas**: Assumption-based archetypes synthesizing existing domain knowledge, organizational context, and behavioral observations before formal field studies.
* **4-Quadrant Empathy Maps**: Capture a single user persona's current-state experience:
** **SAYS**: Direct verbal statements, quotes, and explicit questions vocalized aloud.
** **THINKS**: Unspoken anxieties, doubts, preoccupations, and internalized beliefs.
** **DOES**: Concrete physical actions, workflows, workarounds, and observable habits.
** **FEELS**: Emotional states, affective adjectives, cognitive fatigue, and emotional triumphs.
** **PAINS & GAINS**: Synthesized obstacles (risks, wasted time) vs. desired outcomes (success metrics, relief).

== Comprehensive Strategic Tech Stack Matrix

The table below maps each persona's operational needs directly to the technical architecture decisions made in the Kaiju codebase:

|===
| Persona & Role | Primary Driver | Empathy Pain Point | Kaiju Tech Stack Alignment

| *Maya Lin (24)* +
`STANDARD_USER` (Public)
| Spontaneous local impact in 2-3 hour weekend slots.
| 8s mobile page load times, phantom shifts, mandatory account walls.
| * *JTE SSR*: Sub-50ms TTFB & 0MB JS bundle. +
* *PostGIS `ST_DWithin`*: Truthful geodesic radius. +
* *STI `OPEN_DOOR`*: Immediate directions without booking. +
* *`ProjectSearchCard` DTO*: Ultra-lean mobile wire payload.

| *David Chen (31)* +
`STANDARD_USER` (Specialist)
| Professional skill application (GIS/Data) & cause dedication.
| Relegated to manual busywork; distance search hiding regional causes.
| * *`tags` & `shift_tags`*: Multi-tag faceted queries (`#gis`, `#cpr`). +
* *`REGIONAL` STI*: Bypasses point radius for regional initiatives. +
* *Volunteer Passport*: Reusable credential screening profile. +
* *Keyset Shifts*: Sub-ms recurring schedule streaming.

| *Chloe Torres (17)* +
`STANDARD_USER` (Student)
| 100 verified hours for college admissions & club group projects.
| Lost paper logs; non-profits delaying hour verification.
| * *Verified Hours Ledger*: Schema tracking verified hours & EIN. +
* *Group Reservations*: Slot block holds with join tokens. +
* *Digital Minor Consent*: Automated parental waiver e-signatures. +
* *Common App PDF Generator*: Verifiable PDF certificates.

| *Raymond Miller (35)* +
`STANDARD_USER` (Mandated)
| Court restitution & SNAP ABAWD monthly work compliance.
| Turned away at door; lost paper logs risking probation violation.
| * *Compliance Attribute Flags*: `accepts_court_mandated`, `snap_work_eligible`. +
* *Tamper-Proof Audit Logging*: GPS geofenced check-in/out timestamps. +
* *Court-Admissible PDF Export*: Official reports with EIN & QR token. +
* *Flexible Keyset Shifts*: Evening/weekend shifts around hourly jobs.

| *Marcus Vance (38)* +
`ORG_ADMIN` (Operations)
| Filling shift capacities; managing 5 hubs without spreadsheet chaos.
| 30–50% volunteer no-shows; duplicate event creation; paper clipboards.
| * *Compose Multiplatform Admin*: Desktop-grade calendar builder. +
* *`project_locations` (1:N)*: 1 umbrella initiative, 10 addresses. +
* *`/no-look` Endpoints*: Fast bulk ingestion for 500+ shifts. +
* *Verified Status Bypass*: Auto-approval for trusted organizations.

| *Dr. Sarah Al-Mansoor (42)* +
`ORG_ADMIN` (Youth/Crisis)
| Youth safeguarding; mandatory background checks; trauma training.
| Unvetted walk-ins; lapsed credentials; catastrophic liability risk.
| * *Gated Roster State Machine*: Shifts locked until `status = QUALIFIED`. +
* *Micronaut Security & RBAC*: Tiered roles & qualification claims. +
* *Credential Expiration Tracking*: Automated 60-day renewal alerts. +
* *Transactional Audit Ledgers*: Append-only background & waiver logs.

| *Elena Rostova (45)* +
`REGION_DIRECTOR` (Civic)
| Municipal safety; clearing review queue under 48h SLA; boundary integrity.
| Review backlogs; ambiguous jurisdictional borders; stealth scope edits.
| * *`administrative_regions` (DDD)*: Preserves civic authority. +
* *48h Escalation Queue*: Hourly cron on partial index escalates. +
* *`PENDING_UPDATE` Trigger*: Edits automatically re-trigger review. +
* *Transactional Audit Ledgers*: Immutable append-only actor diffs.
